Courses

Vocational Courses focus on different skils, On-The-Job Training (OJT) and Entrepreneurship. GVHSS Nellikuth provides 5 different courses.

Lab Technician-Pharma,Biologics & Medical Devices:Wet Lab

English, ED, LTPM, Physics, Chemistry, Biology

The Lab Technician - Pharma, Biologics & Medical Devices: Wet Lab course is a specialized, job-oriented skill development program designed to train individuals for Research & Development (R&D) and Quality Control (QC) operations.

Office Operations Executive

English, ED, OFE, Accountancy, Business Studies, Management

Course trains individuals to manage daily administrative, clerical, and support roles within an organization. It builds practical skills to streamline workflows, handle communication, and optimize business operations.

Junior Software Developer

English, ED, JSD, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics

A junior software developer course equips you with entry-level coding, debugging, and problem-solving skills to launch a tech career, focusing on Python programming language, Relational Database using MySQL, and the software development life cycle (SDLC).

Accounts Assistant

English, ED, AAT, Accountancy, Business Studies, Management

An Accounts Assistant course equips you with the practical skills needed to support an organization's finance team. It covers essential topics like bookkeeping, accounts payable/receivable, payroll processing, and accounting software.

Organic Grower

English, ED, ORG, Physics, Chemistry, Biology

It covers soil health, natural pest management, crop rotation, and organic certification. These programs are designed for aspiring farmers, rural youth, and agricultural entrepreneurs who want to produce healthy food while protecting the environment.

About GVHSS Nellikuth

Government Vocational Higher Secondary School Nellikuth is a historic, co-educational institution in Malappuram, Kerala. Established in 1900 and operating for over a century, the school upgraded to an Upper Primary in 1961, a High School in 1980, and a Vocational Higher Secondary center in 1994. Known for its strong academic track record, the institution regularly achieves highly commendable results.
