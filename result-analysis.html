About GVHSS Nellikuth

Government Vocational Higher Secondary School Nellikuth is a historic, co-educational institution in Malappuram, Kerala. Established in 1900 and operating for over a century, the school upgraded to an Upper Primary in 1961, a High School in 1980, and a Vocational Higher Secondary center in 1994. Known for its strong academic track record, the institution regularly achieves highly commendable results.
