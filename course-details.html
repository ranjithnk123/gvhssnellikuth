Skill development courses are specialized, job-oriented programs designed to build practical, in-demand technical and soft skills. Whether you are a student or a working professional, they bridge the gap between academic education and industry requirements to help you get hired fast.

Technology & IT: Coding, AI/Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity.

Business & Marketing: Digital Marketing, Data Analytics, Content Writing, and Financial Modeling.

Healthcare & Wellness: General Duty Assistant (GDA), Child Care, and Personal Training.

Vocational Higher Secondary Education (VHSE) in Kerala integrates Plus Two education with NSQF-aligned job-oriented skill training across 41 trades. Students can pursue specialized fields such as General Duty Assistant, Hand Held Device Technician, GST Assistant, and Graphic Designer, equipping them with professional skills and practical training.

VHSE courses are grouped across various disciplines tailored to local industrial and service sectors:

Engineering & Technology (Science): Field Technician Computing and Peripherals, Optical Fibre Technician, Auto Service Technician, Bio-Medical Equipment Assistant.

Health & Agriculture (Science/Biology): Beauty Therapist, Frontline Health Worker, General Duty Assistant, Micro Irrigation Technician.

Commerce & Business: Sales Associate, Business Correspondent/Facilitator, Office Operations Executive, GST Assistant.

Humanities: Customer Service Executive, Dietetic Aide.

About GVHSS Nellikuth

Government Vocational Higher Secondary School Nellikuth is a historic, co-educational institution in Malappuram, Kerala. Established in 1900 and operating for over a century, the school upgraded to an Upper Primary in 1961, a High School in 1980, and a Vocational Higher Secondary center in 1994. Known for its strong academic track record, the institution regularly achieves highly commendable results.
